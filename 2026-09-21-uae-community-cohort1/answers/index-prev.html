An exosome is a tiny carrier made by cells. Lemonderma's exosome comes from Jeju lemon juice, and we make it ourselves at our lab, Zishel Bio.
따끔함이 꽤 오래가요. 괜찮은가요?John가벼운 따끔함은 보통 몇 분 안에 사라집니다. 비건 마이크로 크리스탈이 작용하는 느낌이며, 자극에 의한 화끈거림이 아닙니다. 그래도 불편하시면 이틀에 한 번으로 줄여 보시고, 계속 불편하시면 사용을 멈추고 저희에게 바로 알려 주십시오.
A light tingle usually fades within a few minutes. It is the vegan microcrystal working, not a burn from irritation. If it still bothers you, use it once every two days, and if it continues, stop and let us know right away.

You can use just one. But the serum opens the way and the cream seals it in, so they work best together.

Yes, you can use it with your usual essence or cream: serum first, then your product, and Lemonderma cream last. With strong products like retinol or exfoliants, we suggest using them on different days.
